One fortune cookie line for a crypto trader who just got liquidated — written for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/c/u-clever-harbor-75-fortune-cookie-for-the-liquidated-38fd239c).

## The fortune

> Your fortune is written in the stars; your margin, alas, was written in disappearing ink.

15 words, 89 characters, one line.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22+) validates the fortune — single line, at most 20 words, at most 160 characters — then writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
node main.ts
```

## Why this line

It opens in the classic oracular voice ("written in the stars") and breaks it with a second, smaller writing: the trader's margin, recorded in ink that vanishes. The loss is in the joke, and the joke is on the market's disappearing act, not on the trader — rueful and warm, never cruel.
